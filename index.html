Hello and Welcome! My name is Kirsty.

I am a 13 year old puppet show researcher, and I am currently researching Popcorn Park.

More about me: I like puppetry, sculpting, drawing, and anything related to arts.

The reason why I started researching about puppet shows is that I grew up with them. And so, I got interested on how they are built, who built them, and how they started. And so, when I turned 9, I started researching about Eureeka's Castle, and then Popcorn Park. And I am looking forward to learning more puppet shows.
